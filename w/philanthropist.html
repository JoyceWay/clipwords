philanthropist

n. /fɪˈlænθrəpɪst/

a person who gives money or time to help others

慈善家

“Genius, billionaire, playboy, philanthropist.”
Tony Stark · The Avengers (2012) · PG-13
▶ Watch the clip · 看片段

WHAT'S GOING ON · 片段解读

Break it down: phil- (love) + anthrop- (humankind). Asked what he is without the suit, Tony lists four titles.

拆开记：phil-（爱）+ anthrop-（人类）。美国队长问他脱了战甲还剩什么，Tony 一口气报了四个身份。

EXAMPLE · 例句

The philanthropist funded scholarships for first-generation college students.

这位慈善家为家里第一代上大学的学生设立了奖学金。
