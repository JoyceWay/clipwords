metaphor

n. /ˈmetəfɔːr/

describing one thing as if it were another, not meant literally

隐喻；比喻

“It's just a metaphor, dude. Metaphors are gonna go over his head.”
Peter Quill / Rocket · Guardians of the Galaxy (2014) · PG-13
▶ Watch the clip · 看片段

WHAT'S GOING ON · 片段解读

“Go over his head” is itself a metaphor for “he won't get it.” Drax takes it literally and says his reflexes are too fast, which proves Rocket's point.

“go over his head”本身就是比喻，意思是“他听不懂”。Drax 却按字面理解，说自己反应快，飞过头顶的东西都能接住。这正好说明什么叫 metaphor 和 literal（字面的）。

EXAMPLE · 例句

The poet's metaphor of a “paper sky” suggests how fragile the speaker's hopes are.

诗人用“纸做的天空”这个隐喻，暗示说话人的希望有多脆弱。
