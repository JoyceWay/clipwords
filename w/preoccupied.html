preoccupied

adj. /priˈɑːkjəpaɪd/

so focused on one thing that you ignore others

全神贯注的；只顾着想某件事的

“Your scientists were so preoccupied with whether or not they could…”
Ian Malcolm · Jurassic Park (1993) · PG-13
▶ Watch the clip · 看片段

WHAT'S GOING ON · 片段解读

The rest of the line: they never stopped to ask whether they should. They focused on “could” and ignored “should.”

后半句是：他们没停下来想想“该不该”做。只顾着“能不能”，忽略了“该不该”。

EXAMPLE · 例句

Preoccupied with her phone, she rode right past her stop.

她一直盯着手机，坐过了站。
