solemnly

adv. /ˈsɑːləmli/

in a very serious and formal way

庄严地；郑重地

“I solemnly swear that I am up to no good.”
The Weasley twins · Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban (2004) · PG
▶ Watch the clip · 看片段

WHAT'S GOING ON · 片段解读

The joke is the contrast: an oath-like, serious tone for promising to cause trouble. Solemn often goes with oath, promise and ceremony.

笑点在反差：用宣誓一样郑重的语气，说“我要去干坏事”。solemn 常和 oath、promise、ceremony 搭配。

EXAMPLE · 例句

The new citizens solemnly pledged to uphold the constitution.

新入籍的公民郑重宣誓维护宪法。
