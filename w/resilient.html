resilient

adj. /rɪˈzɪliənt/

able to survive damage or bounce back quickly

有韧性的；打不垮、能很快恢复的

“What is the most resilient parasite? …An idea. Resilient. Highly contagious.”
Cobb · Inception (2010) · PG-13
▶ Watch the clip · 看片段

WHAT'S GOING ON · 片段解读

Cobb says the most resilient “parasite” is an idea: once it takes hold, it's almost impossible to get rid of. Listen for eradicate right after it, another SAT word.

Cobb 说最顽强的“寄生虫”是一个想法：一旦在脑子里扎根，几乎没法清除。片段里紧接着还有 eradicate（根除），也是 SAT 词。

EXAMPLE · 例句

Coral reefs proved more resilient than scientists expected, recovering within a decade.

珊瑚礁比科学家预想的更有韧性，十年之内就恢复了。
