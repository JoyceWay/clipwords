merely

adv. /ˈmɪrli/

only; nothing more than

仅仅；只不过

“Oh, you think darkness is your ally? You merely adopted the dark. I was born in it.”
Bane · The Dark Knight Rises (2012) · PG-13
▶ Watch the clip · 看片段

WHAT'S GOING ON · 片段解读

Bane is saying Batman only chose the dark later in life, while he was born in it. Merely adds a dismissive “that's all” tone.

Bane 的意思是：你只不过是后来才选择了黑暗，我生来就在黑暗里。merely 带一点“不过如此”的轻视语气。

EXAMPLE · 例句

The author argues that the new rule is merely symbolic and changes nothing in practice.

作者认为新规定只不过是象征性的，实际上什么也没改变。
