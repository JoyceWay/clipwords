ironic

adj. /aɪˈrɑːnɪk/

the opposite of what you would expect

有讽刺意味的；事与愿违的

“Ironic. He could save others from death, but not himself.”
Palpatine · Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith (2005) · PG-13
▶ Watch the clip · 看片段

WHAT'S GOING ON · 片段解读

He could save others but not himself. The outcome is the opposite of what you'd expect, and that is irony.

能救别人，却救不了自己。结果和预期正好相反，这就是 ironic。

EXAMPLE · 例句

It is ironic that the fire station itself burned down.

具有讽刺意味的是，消防站自己被烧毁了。
