inconceivable

adj. /ˌɪnkənˈsiːvəbl/

impossible to imagine or believe

难以想象的；不可思议的

“Inconceivable! You keep using that word…”
Vizzini / Inigo · The Princess Bride (1987) · PG
▶ Watch the clip · 看片段

WHAT'S GOING ON · 片段解读

Vizzini keeps shouting “Inconceivable!” about things that keep happening. Inigo points out the word may not mean what he thinks.

Vizzini 一直喊“不可能！”，可事情偏偏一次次发生。Inigo 吐槽：你老用这个词，它的意思恐怕不是你以为的那样。

EXAMPLE · 例句

A century ago, video calls across oceans would have seemed inconceivable.

一百年前，跨洋视频通话会被认为是不可想象的。
