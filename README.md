# ClipWords · 片段词

**Grades 6–12 vocab from movies · 电影台词里学初高中词汇**

Website: https://joyceway.github.io/clipwords/
YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/@ClipWordsVocab

Each word comes with a short movie clip (played from YouTube), the line highlighted as the word is said, a bilingual meaning sheet (definition, example, synonyms and antonyms, word roots, common phrases, pronunciation) and an optional quiz.

Three levels:
- Basic · Grades 6–8 · 基础篇
- Intermediate · Grades 9–10 · 中级篇
- Advanced · Grades 11–12 · 高级篇 (covers words that often appear on the SAT®)

Files: `index.html` (the app) and `words.js` (word data).

Clips play from YouTube through the official embedded player; all rights belong to their owners.
